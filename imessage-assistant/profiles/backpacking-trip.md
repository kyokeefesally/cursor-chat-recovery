# Profile: backpacking / group trip planning

A lens over `imessage-summary`. Use for a group chat organized around a trip. Adds
what to track and how to shape the output; everything in the base spec still applies.

## Track these specifically

**Roster** — the single most important thing. For every participant: in, out,
conditional, or unclear; the reason; and the date they last changed. People flip more
than once — the table shows the current state, the timeline shows the flips.

**Destination & route** — where, trailhead, route/loop, mileage, elevation, nights out,
campsite plans. Note when a destination changed; early proposals get abandoned quietly
and a summary that reports the abandoned one is worse than useless.

**Dates & timing** — trip dates, departure time, meeting place and time, return.
Copy these exactly.

**Permits & reservations** — required, applied for, obtained, or still open, and who
holds them. A permit nobody has secured is a headline item.

**Transport** — who's driving, seat and pack capacity, carpool pairings, meeting point,
gas splitting.

**Gear** — the shared/heavy items: tents, stoves, water filters, bear canisters, first
aid, satellite communicator. Track claimed vs. still needed. Personal gear chatter only
if unresolved.

**Food** — the plan (communal vs. individual), who's buying/cooking what, dietary
constraints anyone raised.

**Money** — permit fees, gas, group gear, food. Who fronted what, who owes whom.

**Conditions & risk** — weather, snowpack, river crossings, fire closures, road status,
bears. Include anything that could still cancel or reroute the trip.

**Fitness & shakeouts** — training hikes, injuries, who's worried about pace or
altitude.

## Output adjustments

- Put **Who's in** immediately after **Where things stand**. It's the first thing
  anyone wants.
- Make **At a glance** a real table: Destination · Trailhead · Dates · Depart ·
  Meeting point · Nights · Permit · Est. cost. Write `— not settled` for anything
  unresolved. Do not omit an unsettled row; a blank is information.
- Add a **Gear** subsection under "Who owes what": claimed items vs. still needed.
- Under **Open loops**, lead with anything that blocks the trip happening — permit,
  transport gaps, unconfirmed people occupying car seats.
- Keep **Banter & color** for a trip chat. It's most of the message volume and it's why
  the group is going.

## Watch for

- **The unconfirmed seat.** Someone "maybe" in a car with fixed capacity is a real
  problem that the chat may not have noticed. Flag it.
- **Silent scope changes.** A route mentioned once early, then everyone talks about a
  different one. Report the live one; note the switch.
- **Gear assumed, never claimed.** "Someone's bringing a filter, right?" with no
  answer is an open loop, not a solved problem.
- **Stale decisions.** Dates set weeks ago that later messages quietly contradict.
