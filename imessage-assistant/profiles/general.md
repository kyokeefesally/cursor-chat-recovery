# Profile: general conversation

The default lens when no specific profile fits. Follow the base `imessage-summary`
spec; this only adjusts emphasis.

## Track

- **Decisions and plans** — anything settled, and anything proposed but not settled.
- **Commitments** — who agreed to do something, and by when.
- **Events** — dates, times, places mentioned. Copy exactly.
- **Requests** — asks directed at the user specifically. Surface these; they're the
  most common thing to miss while behind on a thread.
- **News** — life updates people shared. In a personal thread this is often the point.
- **Unanswered questions** — especially ones aimed at the user.
- **Tone shifts** — tension, excitement, someone going quiet after being active.

## Output adjustments

- Drop **Who's in** and **At a glance** unless the thread is organizing something.
- Add a **For you** section directly after **Where things stand** when anything is
  waiting on the user — questions they never answered, asks they never acknowledged.
  This is usually the highest-value part of catching up.
- For a 1:1 thread, the timeline can be lighter; the exchange is easier to follow. For
  a busy group thread, keep it strictly chronological and day-grouped.
